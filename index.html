About Yash Roadways

Gujarat's Backbone of Freight Logistics

Headquartered in Anand — the dairy capital of India — Yash Roadways has been the invisible force behind the country's cold chain for over 15 years. We specialize in temperature-sensitive freight, full truckload hauls, and dedicated fleet solutions for dairy cooperatives, FMCG giants, and food processing companies.

Our branch, Anand Transport, extends our reach further — seamless last-mile and long-haul connectivity across Gujarat and beyond.
