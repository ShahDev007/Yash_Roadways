About Yash Roadways

15+ years of moving India's most critical supply chains

Who We Are

More Than a Transport Company

Founded in Anand, Gujarat — the dairy capital of India — Yash Roadways was built on a single promise: your cargo arrives on time, every time. Over the past 15+ years, we have grown from a small fleet operation to one of Gujarat's most reliable freight partners.

We specialise in cold chain logistics for India's dairy giants, handling everything from Amul's inter-state hauls to last-mile dairy distribution across Gujarat. Our understanding of temperature-sensitive cargo, dairy industry timelines, and long-haul freight management is unmatched in the region.

Through our branch Anand Transport, we extend our services further — covering intra-city distribution, local freight, and short-haul routes with the same precision as our long-haul operations.

Our Branch

Anand Transport

Anand Transport is our dedicated branch operation, extending Yash Roadways' reach into intra-city and short-haul logistics. Based in Anand, Gujarat, this branch serves the immediate freight demands of local dairy plants, FMCG distributors, and food processing units in and around the Anand district.

With the same standards of reliability and professionalism as the parent company, Anand Transport ensures that even local shipments get the full Yash Roadways treatment.
