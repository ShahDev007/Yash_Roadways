Cold Chain

Refrigerated Transport

Our reefer fleet is purpose-built for India's dairy and food industry. We maintain precise temperature control — from 2°C to 8°C — throughout the entire journey, whether it's a short hop across Gujarat or an interstate haul to Delhi.

Every reefer truck is equipped with temperature monitoring, and our drivers are trained specifically for cold cargo handling. When Amul and Mother Dairy need reliability, they call us.

  • ✓ 2°C – 8°C precision temperature control
  • ✓ Continuous temperature monitoring
  • ✓ Trained cold chain drivers
  • ✓ FSSAI-compliant operations

Bulk Freight

Full Truckload (FTL)

For large-volume shipments that need a dedicated vehicle, our FTL service delivers maximum efficiency and security. Your cargo loads first, travels alone, and arrives at its destination without stops or transfers.

We operate a modern fleet of heavy-duty trucks capable of carrying up to 35MT per trip, covering every major freight corridor in India.

  • ✓ Up to 35MT capacity per truck
  • ✓ Direct origin-to-destination haul
  • ✓ 24/7 dispatch availability
  • ✓ Pan-India coverage
📦
Flexible

Part Load (LTL)

Not every shipment needs a full truck. Our LTL service lets you share space with other consignments, dramatically reducing your freight cost while maintaining the same delivery standards.

Ideal for FMCG distributors, food processors, and smaller dairy operations who need regular freight movement without committing to a full truckload every time.

  • ✓ Cost-effective shared loads
  • ✓ Same reliability as FTL
  • ✓ Flexible pickup scheduling
  • ✓ Suitable for 1MT to 15MT

Enterprise

Dedicated Fleet Contracts

For businesses with consistent freight needs, our dedicated fleet contracts offer the best of both worlds: the reliability of an in-house fleet without the capital expenditure. We assign dedicated trucks and drivers to your operations.

Amul, Sumul, and several other major dairy cooperatives operate with Yash Roadways on long-term fleet contracts, trusting us to handle their critical supply chain links.

  • ✓ Dedicated trucks & drivers
  • ✓ Custom SOPs for your business
  • ✓ Monthly/annual contract options
  • ✓ Priority dispatch always
